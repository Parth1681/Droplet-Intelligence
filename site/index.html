What changed

  • All models start independently inside each training fold.
  • Three inner surface folds choose a model without the outer test surface.
  • Interval calibration is also isolated from the outer test surface.
  • Model bundles contain numerical arrays, provenance and integrity hashes.
  • The browser evaluates the actual GP, including posterior uncertainty.

What the data cannot establish

  • Only 12 independent textured surfaces are available.
  • Contact-angle coverage is incomplete. Wettability is not an input to this release.
  • Texture descriptors retain the supplied geometry assumptions.
  • Cross-validation does not guarantee per-surface coverage under distribution shift.
  • New experiments are needed to validate performance after development.

Reproduce the research

The package includes source code, measured data, SEM training pixels, trained CNN weights, all fold predictions, nested selection records, exported models, tests and a local HTTP API. The hosted demo runs inference in the browser; training is performed with Python.

How the images are used

43×, 100× and 350× images pass through a shared convolutional encoder. Their learned features are combined to estimate smooth fraction and texture volume per area. The GP combines these image-derived estimates with Re, We and droplet diameter.

All three images and their augmentations stay together when a surface is held out. REF-H images are excluded from every training fit.

What this experiment establishes

The network is trained on real pixels. Its descriptor targets are the existing geometry-based proxies, not new independent measurements. There are only 12 textured surfaces; nominal GP intervals do not include CNN uncertainty.
