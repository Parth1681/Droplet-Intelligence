      Where it stops working

      The 90% interval holds overall on unseen textured surfaces, but not for every fluid. Thin fluids miss more often. On the smooth plate only 62% of impacts fall inside: it is hydrophobic, the textures are superhydrophobic, and wettability is not an input.

      Experimental data

      The impacts underneath

      1,498 impacts on 12 textured surfaces and 125 on the smooth plate, across five fluids. Filter by surface and composition. Measurements: Može et al. (2025), University of Ljubljana.

      SEM at 43× · 12 textures + 1 smooth plate

      Surfaces as numbers

      Each texture is a grid of laser tracks 50 to 800 µm apart, 6 or 25 µm deep. From SEM, the tracks are about 45 µm wide (deep) and 30 µm (shallow). That gives the flat area fraction φ, and the smooth plate is simply φ = 1. Tap a surface to send it to the model.

      SEM image model · 39 real images

      From microstructure to spreading

      43×, 100× and 350× views pass through a shared convolutional encoder that estimates φ and texture volume. The GP then combines these image derived values with Re, We and D0. All three views stay together when a surface is held out, and REF-H images never enter training.

      What this experiment establishes
      • The network is trained on real pixels. Its targets are the geometry based descriptors, not new independent measurements.
      • Only 12 textured surfaces exist, so the image branch stays experimental.
      • Nominal GP intervals here do not include CNN uncertainty.

      How it was built

      Three steps

      01 · DESCRIBE

      Surface descriptors

      Track width from SEM sets the flat area fraction and the texture volume for every surface, including the smooth plate.

      φ = (max(s − w, 0)/s)²
      Vtex = (1 − φ)·h
      02 · MODEL

      Gaussian process

      Matérn 3/2 kernel, one length scale per input, plus white noise, fitted on ln βmax.

      inputs: ln Re, ln We, ln D₀, φ, Vtex
      03 · TEST

      Hold out whole surfaces

      Leave one surface out picks the model. A change counts only if its 95% bootstrap interval over surfaces excludes zero. The smooth plate is never used for training.

      12 folds · 4,000 bootstrap draws
      Prediction pipeline
      InputDrop, fluid, surfaceD₀, V, ρ, μ, σ and the surface name or descriptors
      FeaturesRe · We · ln D₀ · φ · VtexDimensionless groups plus SEM based surface encoding
      PosteriorGaussian processMean and uncertainty, evaluated in float64
      ChecksRange + anomalyMeasured support, Mahalanobis percentile, smooth surface warning
      What the data cannot establish
      • Only 12 independent textured surfaces are available.
      • Wettability is not an input; contact angle coverage is incomplete.
      • Cross validation does not guarantee coverage for every surface under shift.
      • New surfaces, with a test set fixed before tuning, are needed to confirm the error.
