### A.2 Datasets found
- **Used:** the Može et al. impact CSVs and the 39 SEM images (above).
- **Not usable:** the liquid-film fluorescence videos found during the video search have no drop edge, scale or frame rate, so they were excluded. No real side-view impact video of these surfaces exists in the project, and the video pipeline was tested on synthetic clips only.

### A.3 Models: validated vs proposed
- **Implemented and evaluated** (in Tables 5–6):
  - GP variants: Matérn 3/2 ARD (final), Matérn 5/2, fluid–surface interaction kernel, Laan backbone + GP residual, and a dimensional-input GP (re-run).
  - XGBoost variants M1, M2, M3, M5 and same-input XGBoost.
  - The rest of the 13-model screen: random forest, extra trees, LightGBM, CatBoost, MLP, deep ensemble, FT-Transformer, SVR, kernel ridge, kNN and quadratic ridge.
  - SEM-CNN descriptor encoder + GP; physical SEM reader + GP (image φ, image-only); wettability variants (cos θ input, Lee β<sub>0</sub>).
  - Laan law alone; surface-blind GP.
- **Proposed only** (Table 8, no results claimed): heteroscedastic GP, quantile/conformal XGBoost, wettability-aware models, temporal D(t) models, a live camera loop, and active learning.
- **"LARP":** not found anywhere in the project (files mentioning it: 0), so it is not included.

### A.5 Problems found and how they are handled
1. **The dimensional-input GP scores lower LOSO RMSE than the final model** (0.0372 vs 0.0400). Earlier drafts did not report this. The re-run confirms it, but the paired surface-bootstrap interval [-0.0084, +0.0014] includes zero. The raw encoding also needs an arbitrary REF-H code: REF-H RMSE ranges from 0.058 to 0.375 depending on it. **Handling:** reported in Table 5, Table 6 and §9 as an unresolved alternative.
2. **The Laan-backbone "improvement" was significant only when replicate conditions were resampled** ([-0.0021, -0.0004]). With whole surfaces resampled, the interval is [-0.0030, +0.0001]. **Handling:** the surface result is reported.
3. **Oh redundancy.** The earlier "PIML" input set used Re, We and Oh together, but ln Oh = ½ ln We − ln Re exactly (max error 1.8e-15), and adding ln Oh changes LOSO by 0 to four decimals. **Handling:** explained in §2 and §9.
4. **REF-H was inspected during development.** **Handling:** it is labelled as a stress test, not blind data. It also ranks models differently from LOSO (SVR and kernel ridge do best there), so it is not used for selection.
5. **The 13-model screen saved only LOSO RMSE, not per-impact LOSO predictions.** **Handling:** LOSO MAE and R² for those models are marked n/a. Their REF-H metrics were recomputed from the saved REF-H predictions and match `zoo_table.csv`.
6. **Browser video timing bug.** Frames were indexed by list position, so dropped frames biased V (one run read 1.414 m/s against a true 1.2 m/s). **Handling:** fixed by indexing frames by media timestamps. 5/5 repeat runs now give V = 1.200 m/s.
7. **Interface label bug.** CSS uppercase turned "µm" into "MM" on two labels. **Handling:** fixed.
8. **Speculative wording in earlier text.** A physical explanation of the larger water scatter had no supporting evidence. **Handling:** replaced by the measured replicate scatter (0.026 for water vs 0.009 for 91 wt%).

## E. Model-comparison section
Sections 7 and 8 of the paper. In short:
- **GP vs XGBoost:** the GP beats XGBoost on unseen surfaces (Δ [+0.0053, +0.0282], GP better on 9/12 surfaces).
- **Why the GP wins:** it suits a small, smooth problem, gives a native posterior variance, and degrades gracefully at the edge of the descriptor range.
- **Why XGBoost loses ground:** it captures interactions but has no native uncertainty and extrapolates as a constant.
- **Role of the CNN:** it is a descriptor reader that complements the GP, not a competing β<sub>max</sub> predictor.

- Wide bootstrap intervals.

- Under-coverage for low-viscosity fluids.
